A Love Letter from the Editor

My dearest bride,

There is a particular hush that falls when a veil is lifted, when candlelight catches a strand of pearls, when the one you love takes your hand and says yes. This edit was gathered for those moments. Every piece was chosen for its grace and for the brides who have cherished it before you: tulle that drifts like a whispered promise, gifts that say thank you without a word, and tables that glow with roses long after the last dance.

Wander slowly. Linger wherever your heart does. And may every detail feel as tender, as lovely and as unforgettable as the love you are celebrating.

Yours, with all my love,
