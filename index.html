A Note from the Editor

For the love of every detail

There is a particular hush that falls when a veil is lifted, when candlelight catches a strand of pearls, when someone you love says yes. This edit was gathered for those moments. Each piece has been chosen for its grace and for the warmth of the brides who have adored it before you: heirloom-worthy tulle, gifts that say thank you without a word, tables that glow long into the evening.

Wander slowly. Linger where your heart does. And may every detail feel as lovely as the day itself.
